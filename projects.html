Gradient ML Science Club — President

I lead the Gradient ML Science Club at Gdańsk University of Technology, organising workshops, leading research projects, and invited talks. The club brings together students interested in ML research and practice — from complete beginners to people working on their first research projects. Building a community where ideas are taken seriously and learning is collaborative is something I find as rewarding as research itself.
