About me

I'm a third-year Computer Science student at Gdańsk University of Technology and an Undergraduate Researcher at NASK National Research Institute, where I work on machine learning research. My interests centre on AI safety and computer vision — particularly in contexts where robustness and efficiency matter as much as raw accuracy.

Before NASK, I spent a year as a Machine Learning Engineer at Arex WB Group, building Edge AI solutions for resource-constrained embedded hardware. That experience gave me a practical appreciation for the gap between research benchmarks and what actually ships.

As President of the Gradient ML Science Club at GUT, I organise workshops, lead research projects, and invite practitioners and researchers to speak. Building a community where students do serious work is something I care about as much as my own research.

I'm open to research collaborations and conversations — feel free to get in touch.
